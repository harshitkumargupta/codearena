A street festival has `n` stall spots in a single row. For each spot the organisers know how much money a stall there
would bring in. Fire rules forbid opening two stalls in neighbouring spots. Which spots should be opened to bring in
as much money as possible? Print that largest total; opening no stalls at all gives 0.

### Input
The first line contains `n`. The second line contains `n` integers, the income of spots 1 to `n`.

### Output
Print the largest total income.

### Constraints
- `1 <= n <= 100000`
- `0 <= income <= 10000`
