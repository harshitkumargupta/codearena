A sign painter wants every sign to read the same from left to right and from right to left. Painting a fresh sign is
expensive, but scraping off a single letter is cheap. Given the text of a sign, decide whether it already reads the
same in both directions, or can be made to by removing at most one letter.

### Input
A single line containing the text `s`, made of lowercase English letters.

### Output
Print `YES` if `s` reads the same both ways after removing at most one letter, otherwise `NO`.

### Constraints
- `1 <= |s| <= 200000`
