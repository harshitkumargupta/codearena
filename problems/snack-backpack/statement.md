Before a long hike you lay out `n` snacks. Each has a weight in grams and an energy value, and you have one of each.
Your backpack can take at most `W` grams of snacks. Choose snacks to carry the most energy without going over the
weight limit. Print that largest total energy.

### Input
The first line contains `n` and `W`. Each of the next `n` lines contains a snack's weight and energy.

### Output
Print the largest total energy that fits in the backpack.

### Constraints
- `1 <= n <= 100`
- `1 <= W <= 10000`
- `1 <= weight <= 10000`
- `0 <= energy <= 1000000`
